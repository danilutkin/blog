High-Pressure Turbine Blade

A twisted HPT airfoil with platform and simplified fir-tree root.

Internal serpentine cooling uses three passes with U-turns from root to tip.

Thermal mode emphasizes hotter leading-edge and tip regions while channels remain cool.

Drag to orbit. Pinch or scroll to zoom. The model rotates automatically until you interact.
