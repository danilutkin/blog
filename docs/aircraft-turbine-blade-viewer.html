Aircraft Turbine Blade

• A swept, twisted airfoil blade that extracts or adds energy to compressible flow.

• Root geometry is wider and thicker to carry load into the disk and hub.

• Tip sections are thinner and narrower to reduce drag and secondary losses.

• Operates under extreme temperature, pressure, and cyclic rotational stress.

• Efficiency depends on micron-scale aerodynamic precision across the full span.

• Built from advanced superalloys and high-control manufacturing processes.
